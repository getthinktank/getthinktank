Our product

OurCharter

Governance and membership software for member-run organizations. Elections with vote receipts, minutes, bylaws, committees and documents with an audit trail — plus member communications and a public website for each organization, on the web and in a mobile app.

How we work

Three commitments that shape everything we ship.

01

Records first

A decision you can't reconstruct later didn't really happen. Every action that matters leaves a trail the organization owns.

02

Consent before contact

People hear from their organization because they chose to. No harvested lists, no surprise texts, and an exit that's one tap away.

03

Built for the volunteer

The treasurer has a day job. If it takes a manual to use, we haven't finished designing it.
